# Data export: range, format, includes, images

A Premium user downloads their receipts, line items, categories, budgets and price observations (NFR Privacy 2, V1.1; Premium per decision D2) from `GET /api/users/me/export?format=json|csv|pdf&from=&to=&include=` (options PREM-04, DA-8): one JSON document, a zip of `receipts.csv`, `line_items.csv`, `categories.csv`, `budgets.csv` and `price_observations.csv`, or a PDF spend report. `from` and `to` pick receipts by date (their lines and prices go with them; categories and budgets are never cut); `include` picks the sections (`receipts`, `prices`, `budgets`, and `images`, which makes the download a zip with `images/`). `GET /api/users/me/export/summary?from=&to=` gives the count for "Export N receipts". Deleted receipts, their lines and prices, and another user's data are never in it. A free user gets `403 premium_required` with `feature` `data_export` from both routes, and the server records `data_exported` (format, range, includes, counts) only for an export it built.

## Sub-features

- `export-free` a free user's summary and PDF requests both get `403` with `{"code":"premium_required","feature":"data_export"}`, and no `data_exported` event.
- `export-summary` the summary counts the user's not-deleted receipts in the range (all time 3, the last 12 months 2, an empty year 0); `from` after `to` is `422` `invalid_range`.
- `export-range` the JSON export of a range holds only the receipts dated in it, with their lines and prices, its `range` and `includes`, the budget, and the built-in and custom categories.
- `export-includes` `include=prices` leaves out `receipts`, `categories` and `budgets` (key and all); an unknown include is `422` `invalid_include`.
- `export-images` CSV with `images` is one zip: the five CSV files, `images/<receipt id>.jpg` for the scanned receipt (a JPEG), and `images/README.txt` counting 1 image and 1 receipt with none (the manual one).
- `export-pdf` the PDF report (`%PDF-`) of the range has the range label, the receipt count, "Monthly spend by category" with the month and its categories, "Top items" and "Receipts" (date, store, total).
- `export-all` all time with every switch is all three receipts, all seven prices, the budget and both scanned images.
- `export-scope` the other user's id, receipt and budget, and the user's own deleted receipt, are in none of the files.
- `export-event` each export records `data_exported` with its format, `from`, `to`, `includes`, `receipt_count`, `image_count` and `images_left_out`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Settings → ACCOUNT → Export data. A Premium user sees the intro card, a Range group (Period: Last 12 months, All time, This year, Custom; From and To months), a Format group (CSV, JSON, PDF report), the Includes switches (Receipt images off) and "Export N receipts"; the app downloads the file and opens the iOS share sheet (Save to Files, AirDrop, Mail). A free user sees "Export your data with Premium" with See Premium, which opens the paywall. The screen and the share sheet are UI and need a Simulator check; this journey proves the API they call.

## Driving it with gg-verify

Preconditions: a debug-mode instance; `$G doctor` shows no `FAIL`. `$G download PATH` saves the body raw (`NNN-GET-<path>.body`, next to a `.json` with the status, content type and disposition) and prints its path; `$G api` cannot hold a zip or a PDF. Grant Premium with `$G revenuecat` and the `ev` builder from [premium.md](./premium.md) (its last argument is the app user id). `R='from=2025-12-01&to=2026-11-30'` is the app's Last 12 months in November 2026.

- **Free (`export-free`).** `$G signup export-free`. `$G api GET "/api/users/me/export/summary?$R" || true` prints `-> 403`, `.detail` `{"code":"premium_required","feature":"data_export",...}`. `f=$($G download "/api/users/me/export?format=pdf&$R") || true; jq -c .detail "$f"` is the same 403 body.
- **Another user's data.** `$G signup export-other`, `other=$($G api GET /api/users/me | jq -r .id)`, `orid=$($G scan | jq -r .receipt_id)`, confirm it (its image is stored), and `$G api POST /api/budgets '{"amount":987.65,"period_type":"monthly","period_start":"2026-03-01"}'`.
- **The Premium user.** `$G signup export`, `uid=$($G api GET /api/users/me | jq -r .id)`, `$G revenuecat "$(ev "x-$uid" INITIAL_PURCHASE $now $((now+30*day)) "$uid")"` is `applied`. Scan and confirm one receipt (`rid`, 2026-03-15). Enter `$G api POST /api/receipts '{"store_name":"Corner Shop","date":"2026-03-20","total":3.5,"items":[{"name":"=HYPERLINK(\"x\")","unit_price":3.5,"total_price":3.5}]}'` (`mid`). Scan a third (`old`), `PATCH` its `date` to `2025-06-10`, confirm it. Scan, confirm and `DELETE` a fourth (`gone`, `-> 204`). `$G api POST /api/budgets '{"amount":450,"period_type":"monthly","period_start":"2026-03-01"}'` and `$G api POST /api/categories '{"name":"Pet food"}'`.
- **Summary (`export-summary`).** `$G api GET /api/users/me/export/summary` is `{"receipt_count":3}`; with `?$R` `{"receipt_count":2}`; `?from=2020-01-01&to=2020-12-31` `{"receipt_count":0}`; `?from=2026-05-01&to=2026-04-30` prints `-> 422` with `.detail.code` `invalid_range`.
- **Range (`export-range`).** `j=$($G download "/api/users/me/export?format=json&$R")` prints `-> 200 (application/json)`. `jq` on it: `range` `{"from":"2025-12-01","to":"2026-11-30"}`, `includes` `["receipts","prices","budgets"]`, `receipts` is `rid` (Test Grocery Store, 2026-03-15, 3 lines) then `mid` (Corner Shop, 1 line), `budgets` `[450.0]`, the categories with `is_default` false are `["Pet food"]`, and 4 `price_observations`. Neither `old` nor `gone` is in it.
- **Includes (`export-includes`).** `p=$($G download "/api/users/me/export?format=json&include=prices&$R")`; `jq -c keys "$p"` is `["currency","exported_at","format_version","includes","price_observations","range"]`. `$G api GET '/api/users/me/export?include=receipts,photos' || true` prints `-> 422` with `.detail.code` `invalid_include`.
- **Images (`export-images`).** `z=$($G download "/api/users/me/export?format=csv&include=receipts,prices,budgets,images&$R")` prints `-> 200 (application/zip)`. `unzip -l "$z"` lists `budgets.csv`, `categories.csv`, `images/$rid.jpg`, `images/README.txt`, `line_items.csv`, `price_observations.csv`, `receipts.csv`. `unzip -p "$z" images/README.txt` reads "1 receipt image, …" and "1 receipt has no image: entered by hand, or the image is no longer stored." `unzip -p "$z" "images/$rid.jpg" | head -c 3 | od -An -tx1` is `ff d8 ff` (a JPEG).
- **PDF (`export-pdf`).** `d=$($G download "/api/users/me/export?format=pdf&$R")` prints `-> 200 (application/pdf)` and starts `%PDF-`. Its text (`backend/.venv/bin/python -I -c "import sys, pypdfium2 as p; d=p.PdfDocument(sys.argv[1]); print('\n'.join(d[i].get_textpage().get_text_range() for i in range(len(d))))" "$d"`): "Spend report", "Dec 2025 – Nov 2026", "2 receipts · Exported …", "Monthly spend by category", "March 2026 $18.27" with Household $8.49, Groceries $6.28 and Uncategorized $3.50, "Top items" (Paper Towels 6pk first, $8.49), and "Receipts" with `2026-03-15 Test Grocery Store $15.95` and `2026-03-20 Corner Shop $3.50`.
- **All time (`export-all`).** `a=$($G download "/api/users/me/export?format=json&include=receipts,prices,budgets,images")` is a zip with 2 `images/*.jpg` (`rid` and `old`) and one `.json`, whose `range` is `{"from":null,"to":null}`, with 3 receipts, 7 prices and 1 budget.
- **Scope (`export-scope`).** `grep -c` for `$other`, `$orid`, `$gone` and `987.65` in `$j`, in `$d`, in `unzip -p "$z"` and in `unzip -p "$a"`: 0 each.
- **Event (`export-event`).** `$G sql "SELECT properties->>'format', properties->>'from', properties->>'to', properties->'includes', properties->>'receipt_count', properties->>'image_count', properties->>'images_left_out' FROM analytics_events WHERE user_id = '$uid' AND event_name = 'data_exported' ORDER BY created_at"` is five rows: `json|2025-12-01|2026-11-30|["receipts", "prices", "budgets"]|2|0|0`, `json|…|["prices"]|2|0|0`, `csv|…|["receipts", "prices", "budgets", "images"]|2|1|1`, `pdf|…|["receipts", "prices", "budgets"]|2|0|0`, and `json|||[… "images"]|3|2|1`. The free user has none.

## Gotchas

- Grep for ids, not amounts: a random UUID or timestamp can hold `999` by chance, which is why the other user's budget is `987.65`.
- `$G download` exits non-zero on a status of 400 or more, like `$G api`; add `|| true` when the refusal is the expected result.
- The journey sets `old`'s date with one `PATCH` and confirms it with another, as the review screen saves a corrected date before Confirm.
- The PDF's top items group by the name printed on the receipt, as the dashboard's top items do; CSV formula escaping does not apply to it (a PDF runs nothing).
- Batched reads for large accounts and ranges (more receipts and price observations than one 500-row page, a PDF past one page), the spooled file closed however the response ends, the `'` prefix for `+`, `-`, `@`, tab and carriage return, a purged image left out, names outside Windows-1252 in the PDF, and the other 422s (bad date, unknown format, empty include) are covered by pytest (`backend/tests/api/test_data_export.py`, `backend/tests/api/test_data_export_options.py`).
