How it works

Three steps. No typing.

01

Scan or import

Point your camera at a receipt, or pick a photo or file. AI reads the store, every item, and every price.

02

Check and approve

Fix anything that looks off, then approve. Nothing lands in your budget until you say so.

What you get at launch

Every receipt sorted. Every dollar counted.

01 · Review

AI reads it, you approve it.

It reads the store, date, every item, quantity, price, tax and total in seconds, and highlights anything it isn't sure about. Tap any field to fix it. Nothing saves until you confirm.

  • Asks you to double-check items over $100 and receipts over $500
  • Warns you about duplicate receipts
  • Learns your corrections for next time
02 · Budgets

Budgets that keep up.

A monthly grocery budget, plus optional limits per category. Every receipt you confirm updates it.

  • Pace line: “On pace · 5% under” or “Ahead of pace · 12% over”
  • The budget month starts on the day you choose
  • 11 budget currencies
03 · Dashboard

See where it all went.

A budget ring, a bar for each category, and a banner when you're over budget. Compare month over month and follow your 3-month trend.

  • Your top 10 items by spend
  • Search receipt history by store and filter by date
  • Edit or delete any saved receipt, and restore it within 30 days
Scan any receipt

Clean scans, every time.

Apple's document scanner finds the edges and crops the receipt, and blurry or dark photos are caught before upload. Import from Photos or Files, or enter a purchase by hand.

Spending sorts itself

8 categories, sorted for you.

Groceries, Household, Personal Care, Beverages, Snacks & Treats, Baby & Kids, Pet, and Other. Recategorize in one tap, and the fix also updates past receipts.

Alerts before you overspend

A heads-up, not a surprise.

Push alerts at 50%, 80% and 100% of a budget, with the amount and days left. Mute any category, and get a weekly summary on the day you choose.

FAQ

Straight answers.

When can I use it?+

We're in private beta and sending TestFlight invites in waves, earliest sign-ups first.

What does it cost?+

It's free. No paid tiers and no in-app purchases.

What happens to my receipt photos?+

Receipt photos are sent securely to Anthropic's Claude to read them. Anthropic doesn't use them to train its models. We keep the photo with your receipt until you delete it.

How is this different from my bank app?+

Bank apps show "$187 at the store." Grocery Genie shows what the $187 bought, line by line, including cash purchases.

Does it work on Android, iPad or the web?+

Not at launch. Grocery Genie is iPhone only.

Which languages and currencies?+

It reads receipts in multiple languages. Budgets work in 11 currencies. Receipts in other currencies are kept but don't count toward the budget.

Which stores does it support?+

Any store that gives you a receipt. No receipt? Add the purchase by hand.
