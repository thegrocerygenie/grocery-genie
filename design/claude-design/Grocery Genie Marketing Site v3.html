FAQ

Straight answers.

When can I use it?+

We're in private beta on iOS and sending TestFlight invites in waves, earliest sign-ups first. The first release covers receipt scanning, price tracking, and household sharing. Pantry and meal planning follow. Android comes after iOS.

What does it cost?+

Free forever for receipt scanning, ledger, basic budgets, and 3-month price history. Premium ($3.99/mo) unlocks the full price history, cross-store comparison, alerts, exports, and recipe matching. Family ($6.99/mo) covers up to five household members. Early members get a year of Premium free.

Will my receipts be private?+

Yes. Receipts are processed on-device for OCR, then encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell or share your data. Anonymized, aggregated price points may inform the community-pricing feature, and that's fully opt-in.

Does it work with email receipts?+

Yes. Forward digital receipts from Amazon, Instacart, Whole Foods, etc. to a private inbox and they're parsed automatically. Photo and paper receipts work too.

Which stores does it support?+

Any store that gives you a receipt. Cross-store comparison gets better as more receipts from your area come in.
