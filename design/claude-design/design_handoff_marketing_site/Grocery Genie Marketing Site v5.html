Features
Scan any receipt

One photo. No typing.

  • Finds the receipt's edges, crops it, and checks it's sharp

Alerts before you overspend

A heads-up before the overspend, not after.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is it free?+

Yes. Grocery Genie is free on iPhone. You can scan up to 20 receipts a month (about five a week) and add as many as you like by hand. Join the waitlist and get a free year of Premium, our upcoming paid plan, when it launches.

What if it reads my receipt wrong?+

You check every receipt before it's saved. Anything the AI isn't sure about is flagged, every field is editable, and it remembers your fixes.

Is my data private?+

Receipt photos are sent securely to Anthropic's Claude to read them. Anthropic doesn't use them to train its models. We keep the photo with your receipt until you delete it. You decide before AI reads your first receipt, and you can delete your account and all its data from inside the app.

Which receipts work?+

Printed grocery receipts from any store, plus photos and PDFs you already have.

Android?+

iPhone first. We'll share Android plans later.
