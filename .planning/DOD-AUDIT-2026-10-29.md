### Document scanner: size and what a plan must cover

**Size: L** (several days). It replaces a 596-line capture screen (`mobile/app/(tabs)/scan.tsx`) and needs the native dev build that does not exist yet (no `expo-dev-client` in `mobile/package.json`, no `eas.json`). A plan needs these steps:
1. **Pick the module.** Check maintenance, Expo SDK 54 config-plugin support, and that it builds with Xcode 27 and the iOS 27 SDK, with the scene-lifecycle plugin and `withPodsMinDeploymentTarget` in place.
2. **Stand up the dev build.** Add `expo-dev-client` and an `eas.json` development profile. This overlaps the native-build blocker. Decide what Expo Go does: keep the `expo-camera` screen as a fallback, or drop Expo Go for Scan.
3. **Map what the custom screen does today, and keep, move or drop each piece:**
   - the AI-consent gate before the first scan (`09c826a`);
   - the offline queue for captures (`70dda16`);
   - the permission-denied state and Choose from Library;
   - hiding the tab bar;
   - the L1 pill copy;
   - the `useFrameQuality` pre-capture blur and light hints (RC-01 AC 2, `422ebaf`).
   The scanner drops the pre-capture hints, so decide whether AC 2 moves to a post-capture check (backend `app/image/preprocessor.py`) or the PRD is amended.
4. **Handle multi-page output.** The scanner returns several pages, but the backend has no multi-page link (box 9). Decide: first page only, stitch the pages, or block on the link.
5. **Wire the image into the existing upload path.** Keep the format, size limits and quality checks. Retake comes with the scanner's own review, which also closes RC-01 AC 3.
6. **Keep the analytics.** `receipt_scan_started` and `receipt_abandoned {stage: capture}` must still fire with the right stages.
7. **Tests:** mock the native module in Jest, rewrite `scanStates.test.tsx` and the frame-quality tests for the new flow, and add RC-01 AC 1 and AC 3 tests.
8. **Docs:** update `docs/device-checklist.md` §2 (`:41`) and the blur-calibration step, and record the decision in the RC-01 PRD.
